Privacy Policy

Lunara · Last updated 6 September 2026

In short: Lunara stores everything you log on your own phone. There is no account, no server, and nothing is uploaded, shared, or sold.

What Lunara collects

Only what you choose to enter in the app:

  • Period start and end dates
  • Flow level
  • Symptoms you select
  • Moods you select
  • An optional nickname

Lunara does not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any device identifier.

Where your data is stored

All of it is stored locally on your device, using your phone's own app storage. Lunara has no user accounts and no backend server. Your health information is never transmitted off your device.

Data sharing

Lunara does not share, sell, rent, or transfer your data to anyone. There are no advertisers, no analytics services, no tracking software, and no third-party SDKs that receive your information.

Internet access

Lunara works fully offline and does not require an internet connection to function.

Permissions

Lunara requests no Android permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other protected feature of your device.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete everything at any time from within the app: open Profile and choose Clear all data. Uninstalling Lunara also removes all stored data. Because nothing is held on a server, there is no external copy to request deletion of.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open Lunara and see what you have logged, so using a device passcode, fingerprint, or face unlock is the main protection. Lunara does not add separate encryption beyond what your operating system provides.

Children

Lunara is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Medical disclaimer

Lunara is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Cycle predictions are estimates calculated from the data you enter, and can be inaccurate, particularly with irregular cycles. Lunara must not be relied on for contraception or to achieve pregnancy. For any health concern, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
