# LUNARA — PLAY STORE LISTING

## Short description (80 char max)
Track your cycle, moods and symptoms. Private, gentle, entirely on your phone.

## Full description (4000 char max)
Every phase. Every feeling. Every you.

Lunara is a quiet, unhurried place to keep track of your cycle and how you feel while you are living it. No streaks to keep up. No pressure. Just a gentle record of what your body is doing, and a little company along the way.

TRACK WHAT MATTERS

Log a period in a couple of taps. Add how you are feeling from ten moods, and what you are noticing from twelve common symptoms. Everything is optional. Log a lot on the days you want to, log nothing at all on the days you do not.

SEE YOUR OWN PATTERNS

Lunara shows you where you are in your cycle and roughly when your next period might arrive. Once you have logged a few cycles, you will start to see your average cycle length, your shortest and longest, and how your moods tend to move across the month. Nothing is unlocked before there is enough of your own data to make it meaningful.

LEARN, WHEN YOU WANT TO

Twenty-two short articles across seven areas: your cycle, what is normal, conditions, birth control, fertility, perimenopause, and when to get help. Written to be read in a few minutes, without jargon and without alarm.

MOVE GENTLY

Twelve simple exercises grouped by how you are feeling, with clear steps and illustrations. Made for cramps, low energy and tense days rather than for performance.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

Lunara has no account, no sign-up and no server. Everything you log is stored on your device and nowhere else. We cannot read it, because it never reaches us. Clear all of it whenever you like, from one button in your profile.

No ads. No subscriptions. No tracking.

A NOTE ON WHAT LUNARA IS NOT

Lunara is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything. Predictions are estimates based on the cycles you have logged and will not be exact, especially if your cycles are irregular. Please do not rely on Lunara as a method of contraception. If something feels wrong, or your cycle changes in a way that worries you, speak to a doctor.

Lunara is here to help you notice things. What you do with what you notice is up to you.
